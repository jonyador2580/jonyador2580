# The Viking Homelands


Vikingia begins where the Vikings began, in Norway, Sweden and Denmark. These three countries are the homelands of the village, and this guide is for the people who live there and for everyone who loves their story.


## Norway


Norway is the land of fjords and longships. From its western coast, Viking ships sailed out to Iceland, Greenland and beyond. The Norwegian spirit in our village is the spirit of the voyage, setting out with courage and coming home with stories.


## Sweden


Sweden is the land of builders and traders. Swedish Vikings travelled the rivers of the east and built trading towns that connected faraway peoples. The Swedish spirit in our village is the spirit of building, making things that last and trading fairly with neighbours.


## Denmark


Denmark is the land of the first Viking towns. Its flat green fields held some of the earliest Viking settlements, where farmers, craftsmen and storytellers lived side by side. The Danish spirit in our village is the spirit of gathering, of a community that welcomes strangers and turns them into friends.


## How to Join


The village is open to everyone. You do not need to be from Norway, Sweden or Denmark to belong here. You only need to bring something to share, a skill, a story, a question or a helping hand.
